DPDPA — INDIA'S DATA LAW

India's own data protection law follows the same idea as GDPR — here's what it means for our Indian employees, customers, and staff data.

Who It Covers

Anyone in India — employees, customers, staff. Any system holding their data falls under it, wherever that system sits.

Built on the Same Idea

Collect only what's needed, keep it secure, and give people control over their own data — the same principles as GDPR.

Fast Reporting, Same Timeline

Breaches involving Indian personal data follow that same fast-reporting expectation — the sooner it's flagged, the better.

This applies to employee records, payroll details, and any Indian staff or customer data — it has to stay inside approved systems only, the same rule as GDPR.
Individual rights: Indian employees, customers, and staff can ask to see, correct, or have their data deleted — DPDPA calls this the right to correction and erasure, the same idea GDPR gives EU individuals.
What this means for you: Keep Indian employee/staff data in approved systems Report a suspected breach immediately Same rule, different country
[PROTECT DATA] GOLDEN RULE: Same idea as GDPR — if you're not sure whether DPDPA applies, treat it like it does. Both exist for the same reason — to make sure personal data is handled carefully, wherever in the world it sits.
