GDPR — EU DATA PROTECTION

GDPR is the law behind every "keep it in an approved system" rule involving EU employees, customers, or partners — here's what it actually means for your daily work.

Who It Covers

Anyone in the EU or EEA — employees, customers, partners. If we hold their data, GDPR applies, no matter where we're based.

What Counts as Personal Data

Names, emails, passport numbers, even location data. If it can identify a person, GDPR treats it as personal data.

The 72-Hour Rule

Any breach involving this data must be reported within 72 hours — that's why reporting fast matters more than reporting perfectly.

This is why employee and customer data has to stay inside approved, contracted systems — pasting it into a public AI tool or sharing it outside those systems can itself be a breach.
Individual rights: anyone whose data we hold can ask to see it, correct it, or have it deleted — these aren't favors, they're legal rights under GDPR.
What this means for you: Keep EU-linked data in approved systems Report a suspected breach immediately Treat employee & customer data like cash
[PROTECT DATA] GOLDEN RULE: If you're not sure whether GDPR applies to what you're handling — treat it like it does. Speed matters more than certainty — reporting something that turns out to be nothing is always the right call.
