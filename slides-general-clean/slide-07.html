Look-Alike Domains

A "0" swapped for "o" in acmecorp.com looks genuine at a glance. Filters often miss it because the domain is valid — go directly to the app instead of trusting the sender line.

Artificial Deadline

Short, urgent time limits are designed to make you act without thinking.

The Link You're Asked to Click

Never click links in emails like this. Go directly to the portal using a saved bookmark or the official app.
