Fake Recruiter Interview

A candidate is interviewed by a polished "hiring manager" who later asks for a deposit or bank details to "process relocation."

AI‑generated recruiters have been used to steal personal and banking data. HR should always verify any recruiter or candidate call through official channels — never via a link in the interview invite.

Vendor Bank‑Change Call

A long‑standing supplier calls — voice matches — to confirm a new bank account before the next payment.

One call can clone a vendor's voice. Always confirm bank changes on the number already on file.

Crew Allotment Change Call

Someone sounding like a seafarer calls crewing to urgently change bank details for a wage allotment.

Allotment fraud steals seafarer wages. Confirm any bank change directly with the seafarer on a known number.

IT Support "Callback"

Someone sounding like your IT contact asks you to read back your MFA code to "fix a login issue."

Real IT never needs your MFA code. The trick bypasses the login screen entirely.

Same rule, every role: HR: use official channels Finance: check bank changes on file Crewing: confirm with the seafarer Never share codes
[VERIFY — EVERY ROLE] GOLDEN RULE: Money, credentials, or a shortcut — confirm via a trusted contact you already have. Ship or shore, it's not just finance.
