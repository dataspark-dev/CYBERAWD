Development — Mature Practice

VERIFIED TRUST, END TO END

Foundations Growing Practice Mature Practice

At full maturity, trust isn't assumed anywhere in the chain — it's verified and provable.

Signed Commits Good Practice

A signed commit proves who actually authored it — unsigned history can be rewritten or spoofed further down the chain.

Signed / Attested Build Artifacts Good Practice

A signed artifact lets anyone downstream verify it was built by your real pipeline, not tampered with in transit.

Unverified Third-Party Actions Risk

A CI/CD plugin or Action from an unverified publisher runs with your pipeline's own privileges — treat it with the same scrutiny as a new dependency.

Access Reviewed Regularly Good Practice

Least privilege decays over time as roles change — review who can deploy, merge, or access secrets on a real cadence, not just when something breaks.

The through-line: foundations (what you write), growing practice (what builds and ships it), and mature practice (proving all of it is genuine) are one continuous chain — not three separate checklists.
Starting today: Sign commits and verify signed artifacts Treat CI/CD plugins like new dependencies Review deploy and merge access regularly, not just after an incident
[VERIFY TRUST] GOLDEN RULE: Nothing in the chain — a commit, a build, a plugin, an access grant — is trusted just because it's familiar. It's trusted because it's verified.
