KNOWLEDGE CHECK — SPOT THE REAL THREAT

Three quick pairs – email, MFA prompt, and work chat. In each pair, one is fake. Click the one you think is the threat.

Click a card to choose
GENUINE
Email A

IT Service Desk — Password Expiry Notice

From: it.support@synergymarinegroup.com — "Your password expires in 7 days. Reset it anytime from the Fleet Portal app.", signed with a standard footer.

Genuine — correct domain, no urgency, directs to the app, standard footer.
FAKE
Email B

IT Service Desk — Urgent: Access Suspended

From: it.support@synergymarinegr0up.com — "Your Fleet Portal access has been suspended. Click below within 2 hours to restore it."

Fake — lookalike domain (gr0up, not group), urgency, direct link.
GENUINE
MFA A

Sign-In Approval — Fleet Portal

Push from Chrome, Singapore — matches your current location and device.

Genuine — matches your sign‑in attempt, location, and device.
FAKE
MFA B

Sign-In Approval — Unknown Device

Push from an unfamiliar country, unrecognised device: "Approve now or your account locks."

Fake — unfamiliar location, threat to force approval, no sign‑in attempt.
GENUINE
Chat A

Teams — Priya Sharma (Finance)

"Can you re-send last month's invoice PDF? I'll log it in the Finance channel."

Genuine — routine ask, visible channel, no link or secrecy.
FAKE
Chat B

Teams — "Priya Sharma" (Finance)

"Approve this payment via the new link before the call — don't mention it in the group chat yet."

Fake — private DM, payment link, request to stay quiet.
[STOP + VERIFY] GOLDEN RULE: If you have to check a domain name closely to see if it's real, that's already a red flag. When in doubt, don't click – go directly to the app or bookmark.
Attackers know you might check the domain. Skipping the check entirely – going direct – removes the risk.
