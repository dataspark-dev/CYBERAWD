Email isn't the only way in — the same trick lands by text, voice and chat.

Short link hides the real address

Synergy never sends the crew portal as a bit.ly link.

Fake urgency

Pressure to tap before you check.

Vishing (Voice)
"IT Service Desk — Fleet Portal"
Incoming Call — Ship Phone
"Hi, this is IT Support about your Fleet Portal — please confirm your password and the code from your phone so we can fix your rotation access."
Caller name can be faked

Display name can say "IT Service Desk" on any number.

Asking for your password or code

Real IT never asks for your password or code.

Work Chat (Teams/WhatsApp)
Teams Chat · "Priya Sharma" (Finance)
Hi, this is Priya from Finance — can you approve this payment via the new link before the call? Don't mention it in the group chat yet.
Private message bypasses the team channel

A real request would be in the shared channel.

Urgency and secrecy together

Legitimate requests don't need silence.

Across every channel: Don't tap links in unexpected texts Hang up, call a number you already have saved Use the visible team channel, not a DM
[STOP — EVERY CHANNEL] GOLDEN RULE: Same tells on every channel — urgency, faked identity, pressure to act. Hang up, close the chat, use a number you already have. Attackers switch channels to lower your guard, but the tells stay the same.
