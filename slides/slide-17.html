Quick Reference

DO'S & DON'TS

No long lists. Just the habits that stop nearly every attack.

Every habit here traces back to one simple rule: STOP → VERIFY → REPORT.

Click a tile to learn why it matters
DO
Lock Your Screen
Lock it the second you step away, even for a minute.
Verify Requests
Call back on a number you already have, not one they just gave you.
Use MFA
With MFA on, a stolen password alone can't get in.
Report Fast
Reporting something that turns out to be nothing is still the right call.
Use Approved USB Drives
Unknown drives can install malware the moment they're plugged in.
Keep Data in Approved Systems
Approved tools have been checked for exactly this kind of risk.
DON'T
Reuse Passwords
One leaked site can unlock every other account that uses that password.
Click Unknown Links
Go directly to the app or use a saved bookmark instead.
Plug In Unknown USB
Treat any USB drive you didn't buy or verify as unsafe.
Paste Into Public AI
That text can leave your control the moment you hit send.
Leave Desk Unlocked
An unlocked screen is an open door, even if you're gone for a minute.
Stay Silent
Silence is exactly what the attacker is counting on.
