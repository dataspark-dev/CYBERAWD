IT Support — Mature Practice

ROTATE, REVIEW, REMOVE

Foundations Growing Practice Mature Practice

At full maturity, privileged access is never 'set and forget' — it's actively rotated, reviewed, and revoked.

Keys / Credentials Rotated On A Schedule Good Practice

A credential that's never rotated is a standing risk that only grows over time — rotate on a real schedule, not just after an incident.

Stale Access Never Reviewed Risk

Access granted for a project that ended months ago is exactly the kind of forgotten door an attacker looks for.

Least-Privilege Access Reviews Good Practice

Regularly confirm every privileged account still needs the access it has — remove what's no longer justified.

Offboarding Access Removed Immediately Good Practice

A departed employee's or contractor's access should be revoked the same day, not queued for a later cleanup.

The through-line: foundations (never share, always verify), growing practice (vault it, don't inbox it), and mature practice (rotate and review it) are one continuous discipline — not three separate checklists.
Starting today: Rotate keys and credentials on a real schedule Review privileged access regularly and remove what's unused Revoke access immediately on offboarding
