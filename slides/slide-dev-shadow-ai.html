SHADOW AI — THE UNAPPROVED TOOLS

Shadow AI — any AI tool used for work that hasn't been reviewed or approved. Chatbots aren't the only one, and your IDE isn't exempt.

Browser AI Extensions

Free “AI assistant” add-ons read every page you open — including internal dashboards and repos. If it isn't approved, remove it.

Personal AI Accounts

A personal account instead of the org's approved Copilot/AI-coding agreement sits outside our data agreements — no protection, no oversight.

AI Meeting Note-Takers

Bots that auto-transcribe send everything said — including architecture and incident-review discussion — to a third-party server.

Unapproved AI Coding Extensions

A free AI coding extension not on the approved list can send your source code, including proprietary logic and embedded secrets, to a third-party server on every keystroke.

Unapproved AI Dependency Scanners

A free “AI-powered” dependency or vulnerability scanner can send your entire package manifest and internal repo structure to a third-party server outside the approved system.

Not sure whether a tool is approved? Ask before using it with company code, customer data, or secrets — asking is always safe.
