SHADOW AI — THE UNAPPROVED TOOLS

Shadow AI — any AI tool used for work that IT hasn't reviewed or approved. Chatbots aren't the only one.

Browser AI Extensions

Free "AI assistant" add-ons read every page you open — including internal systems. If it isn't approved, remove it.

Personal AI Accounts

A personal account instead of the company one sits outside our data agreements — no protection, no oversight.

AI Meeting Note-Takers

Bots that auto-transcribe send everything said — including confidential discussion — to a third-party server.

Translation & Summariser Plugins

Free "summarise this doc" tools upload the whole document to a third-party server — it then sits outside company control.

AI Image/Deepfake Generators

Free tools make fake images of colleagues, vessels or documents. GDPR: uploading crew or customer images to a public tool can itself be a breach.

Not sure whether a tool is approved? Ask IT before using it with company or customer data — asking is always safe.
If you spot one: Stop using it on company data Asking is safe — unchecked use isn't
