Common thread: PANIC & URGENCY
Did You Know?
A 30-second voice clone now needs just seconds of audio from a public video. Nearly every trick in this deck uses the same trigger: panic that pushes you to act before you check.
Verifying an unexpected or urgent request through a second, trusted channel — calling the person back on a saved number, not one they just gave you — quietly defeats nearly every scenario in this deck, from deepfakes to phishing to Shadow AI.
