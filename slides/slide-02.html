Common thread: PANIC & URGENCY
Did You Know?
Generative AI has made deepfake voice and video attacks cheap and fast — a convincing 30-second voice clone can now be made from just a few seconds of audio scraped from a public video or social post. Nearly every tactic in this deck relies on the same trigger — panic or urgency that pushes you to act before you think.
Verifying an unexpected or urgent request through a second, known-good channel — calling the person back on a saved number, not one they just gave you — quietly defeats nearly every scenario in this deck, from deepfakes to phishing to shadow AI.
