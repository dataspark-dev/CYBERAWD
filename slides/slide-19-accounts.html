Every invoice, wire request, and vendor email you check is a moment attackers are counting on you to rush through. A convincing domain or a senior name is often the only thing standing between a normal payment and a fraudulent one.

Vendor Bank-Detail Change
A bank-detail change is not confirmed by the email announcing it — verify on a number you already have before you pay.
Business Email Compromise
A senior name and an urgent, confidential wire request is the pattern to catch, not proof of who's really asking.
Overpayment Refund Scam
Reversing a payment is far harder than pausing before you send one — check your own ledger first.
Attachment Check
A statement needing “Enable Content” to open is the red flag — check the sender's exact domain before opening anything.
Urgency & Pressure
An urgent deadline is not proof a request is genuine — it's the lever attackers pull hardest.
