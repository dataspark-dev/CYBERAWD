HYGIENE IS THE FIRST DEFENSE

Hygiene Data Handling Endpoint Network AI Leakage Golden Rules

Six baseline controls account for most of what separates a resilient IT environment from a breached one.

Hardware MFA (FIDO2) Good Practice

A physical security key can't be phished the way a one-time code can — deploy FIDO2 hardware keys for every privileged account.

Least Privilege / RBAC Good Practice

Every role gets exactly the access it needs to do its job, nothing more — access is granted by role, reviewed regularly, and revoked the moment it's no longer needed.

Zero Trust Architecture Good Practice

No device or network segment is trusted by default, even inside the perimeter — every request is verified regardless of where it originates.

Automated Patch Pipelines Good Practice

Patches roll out on a schedule, not when someone remembers — a known vulnerability with a patch available is the easiest breach to prevent and the hardest to excuse.

Zero credential reuse and exposure-based instant rotation close the loop: no password is reused across systems, and any credential suspected of exposure is rotated immediately, not queued for the next scheduled cycle.
