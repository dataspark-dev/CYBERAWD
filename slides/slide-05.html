DEEPFAKES: SPOT THE SIGNS

The US$25M call wasn't a one-off — it just made headlines. Here's what gives a deepfake away on a live call.

What would you check before trusting an unexpected call like this?
Real Call — Genuine Signs
Natural blinking & movement

The person blinks and moves their head in a normal, human way.

Perfect lip-sync

Mouth movements match the audio exactly, even with fast speech.

Consistent lighting

Lighting changes naturally as the person moves and the background shifts.

"Can you route this through the usual approval process? I know it's tight, but let's not skip steps."
Deepfake Call — Warning Signs
Unnatural blinking

Very long gaps without blinking — a common sign of a deepfake.

Lip-sync mismatch

Mouth shapes lag behind the audio, especially with hard consonant sounds.

Flat, even lighting

The face lighting barely changes, even when the background moves.

"Pay this invoice by 5pm, don't loop in finance yet — I'll explain later."
