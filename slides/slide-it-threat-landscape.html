IT Support — 2026 Threat Landscape

THE THREATS THAT TARGET YOUR ACCESS

The same four forces are reshaping attacks everywhere — here's what each one looks like from inside the help desk and the endpoint fleet.

Ransomware via Endpoint/RDP Risk

A single unpatched endpoint or exposed remote-access port is often the entire foothold — the lockout that follows is just the visible part of a much earlier compromise.

Social Engineering via the Help Desk Risk

A confident caller, a cloned voice, a fake escalation ticket — IT is targeted because a reset or an override is exactly what attackers need.

Privileged Access Sprawl Risk

Standing admin rights, shared credentials, a session left open — most incidents start as convenience, not malice.

Cloud & Endpoint Misconfiguration Risk

A shared folder left open, an endpoint missing a patch, a misconfigured firewall rule — usually a mistake, and it can sit unnoticed for months.

Most misconfigurations and vendor-update compromises are found months after the fact — anything that behaves oddly right after a patch or update is worth reporting immediately.
Starting today: Hardware MFA + least privilege on every privileged account Patch on a schedule, don't wait for a reminder Report anything odd right after a vendor update rolls out
[REPORT] GOLDEN RULE: You can't audit every vendor's update — but if an endpoint or system behaves oddly right after one installs, treat it as suspicious and report it. Most compromises like this are found months later, which is exactly why reporting early limits the damage.
