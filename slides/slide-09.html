WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU PASTE DATA?

When you paste something into a public AI chatbot, your data leaves your control immediately.

You
Public AI Chatbot
Model Training Data

Your text may be used to train future responses.

Some providers use free-tier chats to improve future responses by default — data you paste isn't just read once and forgotten.
Third-Party Servers

Stored outside your company's control, under different policies.

That data now sits under a different company's retention policy and jurisdiction — not ours, and not something we control.
Potential Leak

Logged data could be exposed in a breach or to another user.

AI providers have had real incidents where one user's chat history became visible to another. Assume anything pasted could resurface.
Never paste customer data, financial details, source code, or credentials into a public chatbot.
Use only company-approved AI tools — they're reviewed for exactly this risk.
Before you paste: Check the approved list first Remove names, numbers, and IDs If unsure, don't paste
