PASTING DATA & SHADOW AI

Paste a crew list, contract, or payroll sheet into a public chatbot and it leaves your control immediately — and chatbots aren't the only unapproved AI risk on the team.

You
Public AI Chatbot
Model Training Data

Your text may train future responses — some free-tier chats use your input to improve models, not just read and forget.

Third-Party Servers

Stored outside company control, under another company's retention and jurisdiction — outside our control.

Potential Leak

Providers have exposed one user's history to another. Assume anything pasted could resurface.

Never paste crew records, allotment details, contracts, financial data, or passwords into a public chatbot — whether at your desk or on board.
Use only company-approved AI tools — they're reviewed for exactly this risk.
GDPR: Crew and customer personal data must stay inside approved, contracted systems — pasting it into a public AI tool can itself be a data protection breach. Sensitive data includes crew records, contracts, financial data, customer info, and vessel ops data.

Shadow AI — any AI tool used for work that IT hasn't reviewed or approved. Have you seen any of these on your team?

Browser AI Extensions

Free "AI assistant" add-ons that read and summarise every page you visit, including internal systems — some log what they read. If unapproved, remove it.

Personal AI Accounts

A personal chatbot account instead of the company-approved one sits outside company data agreements — no protection, no oversight.

AI Meeting Note-Takers

Bots that auto-transcribe calls send everything said — including confidential discussion — to a third-party server.

AI Résumé / Interview Tools

Writing helpers often store what you type — including company details — to train models. You can't delete it once sent.

Unapproved Voyage-Planning AI

Free routing tools send vessel position and cargo to a third party outside the approved system.

Translation & Summariser Plugins

Free "summarise this doc" tools upload the whole document to a third-party server — it then sits outside company control.

AI Image/Deepfake Generators

Free tools generate fake images of colleagues, vessels, or documents. GDPR: uploading crew or customer images to a public tool can itself be a breach.

Before you paste — or if you spot one: Check the approved list first Remove names, numbers & IDs Stop using unapproved tools on company data Not sure? Ask IT — asking is safe
