IT Support — Identity & Trust

THE VOICE ASKING FOR A RESET IS NOT PROOF

A 10-second clip can clone a colleague's voice and face for a live call — increasingly aimed at the one process every account depends on: your reset and verification flow.

Cloned Executive Requesting a Reset Risk

A synthetic voice matching a senior leader, asking for an urgent password reset or MFA bypass — the same US$25M-call tactic, aimed at your reset process instead of a wire transfer.

Fake IT Support “Callback” Risk

An attacker impersonates IT support to socially engineer a user, or impersonates a user to socially engineer IT — either direction targets the same process.

Standard Verification, No Exceptions Good Practice

The identity-verification steps for a reset apply regardless of who's asking or how they sound — a senior title is not an exception.

Verify Via a Second, Known Channel Good Practice

Confirm any unusual request through a channel you already trust, never a number or contact the caller themselves provides.
