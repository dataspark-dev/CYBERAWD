Password on a Sticky Note
Never leave passwords written where others can see them. Use a password manager instead.
Locked Screen
Lock your screen (Win+L / Cmd+Ctrl+Q) every time you leave, even for a moment.
Unknown USB Drive
Never plug in a USB drive you don't own or haven't verified. It can install malware silently.
Badge Worn Visibly
Keep your badge visible, and don't let anyone follow you through a secure door.

Simulate an Idle Desk

Watch what should happen when you walk away – an unlocked screen is an open door.

Locking your screen takes 2 seconds. Leaving it unlocked is an open invitation.
Same rule onboard: bridge, ECDIS, and engine-control workstations are just as exposed as an office desk — lock them during a watch handover or whenever you step away.
Before you step away — office or onboard: Lock your screen (Win+L / Cmd+Ctrl+Q) Put away sticky notes & USB drives Wear your badge, don't tailgate others
[LOCK IT] GOLDEN RULE: If you can see it, so can anyone walking past – lock your screen, secure loose items, and never plug in an unknown drive.
Physical access bypasses almost every technical control. A locked screen and a secured desk close that gap in seconds – and it costs nothing.
