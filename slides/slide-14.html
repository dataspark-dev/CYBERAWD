Speak Up

SEE SOMETHING, SAY SOMETHING

Every warning sign we've covered comes down to three simple steps — and four places you can act on them.

STOP VERIFY REPORT

IT Service Desk

First stop for anything technical — suspicious email, account issue, or unfamiliar device. Reach it 24/7 via Fleet Portal or phone.

Report-Phish Button

Fastest for email: one click flags the message for review and removes it. No forwarding needed.

Security Hotline

For urgent or sensitive cases — breach, lost device, or a payment that now feels wrong. Fastest when you need attention now.

Your Line Manager

If unsure who to contact, your manager is a safe default — on board, the Master or DPA (Designated Person Ashore). Wrong channel first is still better than late or never.

Reporting quickly – even if it turns out to be nothing – is always the right thing to do. No blame, ever.
GDPR: A data breach involving crew or customer information must be reported within strict deadlines — the sooner you flag something odd, the more of that window we keep.
