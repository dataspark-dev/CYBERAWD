Development — Phishing Evolution

PHISHING NOW LOOKS LIKE YOUR OWN TOOLCHAIN

Same trick, sharper disguise — now styled as the tools you already trust, and arriving by more than just email.

Fake PR Comment / Review Request Risk

A comment on a real PR, or a fake review request, linking to a credential-harvesting page styled like GitHub/GitLab's own login.

Fake CI/CD Alert Risk

A build-failure or deploy-approval email styled exactly like your CI system, asking you to “re-authenticate” through a link.

Multi-Channel: Chat, Text, Voice Risk

The same trick now also arrives via a Slack/Teams DM, SMS, or a phone call — often referencing a real, current PR or ticket to feel legitimate.

Open the Real Site Yourself Good Practice

GitHub, GitLab, and your CI system never ask you to re-authenticate through a link in a comment, alert, or chat message — type the address yourself instead.

This connects directly to this month's activities: the PR-comment and CI/CD-phishing scenarios in Fault Finding and Decision Room are built on exactly this pattern.
