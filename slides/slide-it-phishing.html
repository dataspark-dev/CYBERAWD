IT Support — Phishing Evolution

PHISHING NOW LOOKS LIKE YOUR OWN TICKETING SYSTEM

Same trick, sharper disguise — now styled as the tools and tickets you already trust, and arriving by more than just email.

Fake Escalation Ticket Risk

A ticket number and internal jargon are not proof — a fake escalation styled exactly like your own ticketing system, asking for a reset or a credential.

Vendor Impersonation Call Risk

A confident caller claiming to be a known vendor, referencing a real contract or system, asking for a reset, an install, or remote access.

Multi-Channel: Chat, Text, Voice Risk

The same trick now also arrives via a Slack/Teams DM, SMS, or a phone call — not just email or a ticket — often referencing something real to feel legitimate.

Verify On Your Own Approved Contact List Good Practice

Confirm any vendor call or escalation on a number or contact from your own approved list — never one the caller or ticket gives you.
