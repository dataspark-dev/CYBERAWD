YOUR CREDENTIALS OPEN MORE DOORS

Foundations Growing Practice Mature Practice

Admin and privileged credentials are worth more to an attacker than almost anything else in the building — treat them that way.

Never Share Credentials Over Chat/Phone Good Practice

Admin credentials are never read aloud, texted, or pasted into chat — not even to a colleague, not even under pressure.

Verification Skipped Under Pressure Risk

A caller who refuses standard identity verification is the reason the verification step exists — no exception for how senior or urgent they sound.

Standard Reset Process Followed Every Time Good Practice

The same identity-verification steps apply to every reset request, regardless of who's asking or how the request arrives.

Privileged Session Left Open Risk

An admin session left logged in on a shared or unattended screen is a standing risk — lock it or log out.

This connects directly to this month's activities: the vendor-impersonation and credential-reset scenarios in Fault Finding and Decision Room are built on exactly this pressure — a confident caller asking you to skip the one step that protects everyone.
Starting today: Never share credentials over chat, phone, or email Follow the same verification steps every time, no exceptions Lock or log out of privileged sessions when stepping away
