THE LAWS BEHIND THE RULES

GDPR and DPDPA are the two laws behind every "keep it in an approved system" rule in this deck — here's what they actually mean for your daily work.

GDPR — EU Data Protection

Protects the personal data of anyone in the EU or EEA — names, emails, even location data. If we handle data for EU crew, customers, or partners, GDPR applies, no matter where we're based. Any breach must be reported within 72 hours.

DPDPA — India's Data Law

India's own data protection law, built on the same idea: protect anyone's personal data, wherever we hold it. It covers any of our systems with Indian crew, customer, or staff data — and breaches follow that same fast reporting timeline.

Same idea, different countries: collect only what's needed, keep it secure, and report fast if something looks wrong.
What this means for you: Keep data in approved systems only Report a suspected breach immediately Not just an IT rule — everyone handling data shares it
[PROTECT DATA] GOLDEN RULE: If you're not sure whether data protection law applies to what you're handling — treat it like it does. Keep it in approved systems, and report fast if something looks wrong.
Both laws exist for the same reason: to make sure personal data is handled carefully and that people are told quickly when something goes wrong. Speed matters more than certainty — reporting something that turns out to be nothing is always the right call.
