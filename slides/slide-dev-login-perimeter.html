Development — Everyday Defence

YOUR LOGIN IS PART OF THE PERIMETER

What a well-protected developer account looks like — your GitHub/GitLab login, your cloud console, and the SSH keys that stand in for a password.

SSH Key Without a Passphrase Risk

An SSH private key with no passphrase is a single stolen laptop away from full account access — every key gets a passphrase, no exceptions.

Hardware MFA on Git Hosting & Cloud Console Good Practice

A hardware key (FIDO2) on GitHub/GitLab and your cloud provider stops a phished password from reaching your repos or infrastructure.

Unrecognised Sign-In to a Repo or Cloud Console Ignored Risk

An unrecognised sign-in to source control or a cloud console is a critical incident — it can mean silent access to code, secrets, or infrastructure.

MFA Push-Bombing on a Dev Account

A flood of approval requests on your git hosting or cloud account you didn't trigger is an attack, not a glitch — tap Deny, not Approve, and report it immediately.

If a prompt looks wrong, deny and report.
