A CLONED VOICE ASKING YOU TO SKIP VERIFICATION

A 10-second clip can clone a colleague's voice and face for a live call — increasingly aimed at whoever can approve a reset, a merge, or a deploy.

Cloned Executive Requesting a Reset Risk

A synthetic voice matching a senior leader, asking for an urgent password reset or MFA bypass — the same US$25M-call tactic, aimed at your reset process instead of a wire transfer.

Cloned Lead Pushing an Emergency Merge or Deploy Risk

A cloned “engineering lead” or manager pushing to bypass review and merge, deploy, or grant access immediately — recognising the face or voice is no longer proof.

Standard Verification, No Exceptions Good Practice

The identity-verification steps for a reset, a merge, or a deploy apply regardless of who's asking or how urgent it sounds — a senior title is not an exception.

Verify Via a Second, Known Channel Good Practice

Confirm any unusual request — a reset, a merge, a credential — through a channel outside the call itself, before acting.
