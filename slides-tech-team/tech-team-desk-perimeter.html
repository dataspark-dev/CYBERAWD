YOUR DESK IS PART OF THE PERIMETER

An unlocked workstation often has an active admin session, terminal, or SSH agent already signed in — four things worth noticing here specifically, plus what should happen the moment you walk away.

Active Admin Session or Terminal to Prod Risk

An open admin console or a terminal with an authenticated session to production is a bigger prize than a normal desktop login — lock it every time, even for a moment.

Screen Locked Every Time Good Practice

Lock your screen (Win+L / Cmd+Ctrl+Q) every time you step away, whether you're mid-incident, mid-deploy, or just grabbing coffee.

Unknown USB Drive Risk

Never plug in a USB stick you don't own or haven't checked — on these machines it can also reach an active SSH agent, admin session, or cloud credential cache.

Sessions Not Left Forwarded/Idle Good Practice

An SSH agent with forwarding enabled, or an admin session, left active on a machine you've stepped away from can be used by anyone with access to it — lock the screen, and don't forward agents to machines you don't fully trust.

Locking takes 2 seconds — leaving a workstation unlocked with an active session is an open invitation, the same as leaving the credential itself lying around.
Starting today: Lock your screen every time, even mid-incident or mid-deploy Treat an open terminal or admin console like an open vault Don't forward sessions or agents to machines you don't fully trust
