PASTING DATA INTO PUBLIC AI

Paste a ticket, a stack trace, or a customer record into a public chatbot for a quick answer, and it leaves your control immediately.

You

Model Training Data

Your text may train future responses — not just be read and forgotten.

Third-Party Servers

Stored under another company's retention rules and jurisdiction.

Potential Leak

Providers have exposed one user's history to another. Assume it could resurface.

Never paste a ticket, an incident log, a credential, an API key, a .env file, or customer records into a public chatbot — even to debug or troubleshoot faster.
Use only company-approved AI tools for troubleshooting or coding assistance — they're reviewed for exactly this risk, including where your code and data actually go.
GDPR: Customer and employee personal data in a ticket, log, or bug report must stay inside approved, contracted systems — pasting it into a public AI tool can itself be a data protection breach.
Before you paste: Check the approved AI tool list first Redact or strip secrets, credentials & real customer data before pasting anything If unsure, don't paste
